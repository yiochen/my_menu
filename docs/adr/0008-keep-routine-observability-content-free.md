# Keep routine observability content-free

Routine client and server logs will contain only sanitized operation metadata,
job identifiers, timing, status or error codes, model identifiers, and token
usage, and will expire after 30 days. Prompts, menu text, photos, filenames,
signed URLs, and provider responses are excluded; content may be shared for a
specific support case only through a diagnostic bundle the person explicitly
reviews and submits.
