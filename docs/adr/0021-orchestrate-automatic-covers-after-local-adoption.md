# Orchestrate automatic Covers after local adoption

Photo grouping remains an independent processing job, and automatic Cover
generation starts only after its new-Dish outcome is locally adopted or
resolved; Add Idea creates its Dish directly and starts the same separate Cover
job after the local save. A newly eligible Dish receives one automatically
adopted, validated, locally undoable Cover opportunity, while established Dishes
never refresh automatically. This separation keeps organization fast and
reliable while allowing Cover failures, retries, corrections, and quota to
evolve independently.
