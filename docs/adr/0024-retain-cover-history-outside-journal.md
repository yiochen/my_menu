# Retain generated Cover history outside the Journal

Every valid delivered generated image remains in device-local Cover history,
including declined and superseded Proposed Covers, until explicitly deleted.
Generated Covers never appear in the documentary Journal, never become Sources,
and never serve as generation inputs; people may switch offline among history
and Sources. Deleting the selected asset clears the Cover to a non-media
placeholder instead of choosing another image silently.
