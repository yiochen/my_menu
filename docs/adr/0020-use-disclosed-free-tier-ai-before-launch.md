# Use disclosed free-tier AI before launch

During private pre-launch testing, MyMenu may use Gemini's free tier even
though Google may use inputs and outputs to improve its products and human
reviewers may process them. MyMenu must disclose that handling through
versioned consent and warn testers not to submit sensitive, confidential, or
personally identifying content; public launch remains gated on moving to a
provider configuration that does not train on customer inputs and restoring a
server-side deployment attestation.
